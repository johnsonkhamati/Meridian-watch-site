The movement

Every part earns its place.

No decoration for its own sake. The caliber inside every Meridian is chosen for accuracy and repairability, not for what it looks like through a display case back.

01

Automatic caliber

Self-winding movement with a 42-hour power reserve, regulated by hand over five days before it ships.

02

Sapphire crystal

Scratch-resistant to nearly anything short of another sapphire, with anti-reflective coating on both faces.

03

100m water resistance

Screw-down crown and case back tested to depth before final assembly, not just on paper.

04

316L steel case

Surgical-grade steel, brushed by hand in a single direction to catch light the same way every time.

05

±1 second accuracy

Chronometer-level regulation, verified daily, printed on a card that ships inside the case.

06

25-year service plan

One movement overhaul included, whenever it's needed, for as long as you own the watch.
